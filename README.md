Abstract
We present a physically motivated model of the cosmic microwave background (CMB) based on
the concept of a structured vacuum. This approach assumes that vacuum itself possesses an
intrinsic energy structure, capable of generating large-scale patterns in spacetime. We compare
the theoretical spectrum derived from this model to real Planck SMICA data. After appropriate
filtering and residual analysis, we find that the model reconstructs the core geometry of the CMB
remarkably well, suggesting that vacuum structure may play a fundamental role in cosmic
evolution.
